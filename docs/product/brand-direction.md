# Sartoria Brand Direction

## Brand idea

Sartoria represents cultivated personal style through restraint, fit, proportion, and continuity. It should feel like a discreet private service rather than a fashion feed or shopping platform.

## Name and slogan

- Name: Sartoria
- Slogan: **Elegantia in Simplicitate**
- Meaning in use: elegance is achieved through clarity, proportion, and thoughtful reduction

## Logo direction

The approved direction remains crown-based with an old-money Italian-chic character.

The crown should communicate stewardship, confidence, and quality without becoming heraldic, royal-costume, or visually loud. It must remain recognisable at app-icon size and work in one colour.

## Visual principles

- restrained luxury rather than conspicuous luxury;
- editorial spacing and strong typography;
- warm, tactile neutrals supported by deep navy and considered metallic accents;
- subtle depth and material cues rather than glossy effects;
- high-quality garment photography as the main visual content;
- calm motion with purposeful transitions;
- minimal iconography and low interface noise.

## Avoid

- trend-led gradients;
- generic AI sparkle language;
- excessive gold;
- ornate crests;
- fashion-magazine clutter;
- gamification that trivialises personal data or purchasing;
- dark patterns that push users toward buying more.

## Voice

Sartoria speaks with calm confidence. Advice is direct, respectful, and specific. It explains proportion, colour, texture, fit, occasion, and wardrobe context without judgement.

Preferred language:

- “This works because…”
- “A stronger option from your wardrobe is…”
- “You already own the role this item would fill.”
- “This purchase would add…”
- “The main limitation is…”

Avoid superiority, body judgement, urgency, artificial scarcity, and unqualified certainty.

## Experience signature

The product should feel personal from the first meaningful action. It remembers preferences, explains recommendations, reduces decisions, and quietly improves wardrobe coherence over time.

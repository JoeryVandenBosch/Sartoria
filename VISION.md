# Sartoria Vision

## Product promise

Sartoria turns a fragmented wardrobe into a coherent personal style system. It helps each user know what they own, understand what suits them, create confident outfits, identify genuine wardrobe gaps, and avoid purchases that add cost without adding value.

## Primary users

Sartoria initially serves style-conscious adults who value quality and refinement but do not want to spend significant time managing clothing or following trends.

The first product experience is optimised for users who:

- own a mixture of premium, everyday, and occasion clothing;
- want better combinations from their existing wardrobe;
- care about fit, proportion, quality, and consistency;
- need practical guidance for work, travel, social events, and seasonal changes;
- prefer calm, direct recommendations over fashion-content overload.

## Core outcomes

A successful user can:

1. build an accurate digital wardrobe with minimal friction;
2. receive outfit suggestions grounded in owned items;
3. understand why an outfit works or does not work;
4. prepare outfits for an occasion, trip, or weather condition;
5. identify missing essentials and redundant purchases;
6. maintain personal fit, colour, brand, and style preferences over time;
7. make purchase decisions that strengthen the wardrobe as a system.

## Experience principles

- Personal before fashionable
- Existing wardrobe before new purchases
- Explainable before magical
- Refined before decorative
- Useful before social
- Private by default
- Human control over automated decisions

## Initial scope

The first product release includes:

- account and profile foundation;
- wardrobe item capture and organisation;
- item attributes, imagery, fit notes, and ownership status;
- personal style and preference profile;
- outfit creation and recommendation;
- occasion and travel planning;
- wardrobe gap and duplication insights;
- explainable AI recommendations;
- secure deletion and data export foundations.

## Explicit non-goals for the first release

- public social network;
- influencer or advertising feed;
- open fashion marketplace;
- resale logistics;
- virtual try-on presented as precise physical simulation;
- autonomous purchasing;
- medical or body-image assessment;
- recommendations based solely on trends or sponsored inventory.

## Brand direction

The identity remains crown-based, premium, Italian-chic, and restrained. The slogan is **Elegantia in Simplicitate**. The product should feel considered and timeless, not theatrical, nostalgic, or costume-like.

## Success criteria

The product is successful when users repeatedly return to plan real outfits, trust the reasoning behind recommendations, add meaningful wardrobe data, and report fewer low-value clothing purchases.
